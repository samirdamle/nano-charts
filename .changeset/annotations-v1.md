Add `annotations` to `line`, `area`, `lines`, `bar`, `scatter`, `donut`, and `gauge` (and their React components, which pass the option through): an array of marks laid on top of the chart's own marks, in the chart's data coordinates — `{ x, y, shape, color?, size?, emoji?, orientation? }` with `shape` one of `'dot' | 'square' | 'triangle' | 'line' | 'emoji'`. On `line`/`area`/`lines`, `x` is the data index (fractional indices interpolate) and `y` the data value on the chart's domain (shared across series on `lines`, no series targeting in v1); on `bar`, `x` is the category index at the category center and `y` the value on the shared domain (horizontal bars keep logical `x`/`y`); on `scatter`, `x`/`y` are data values; on `donut`/`gauge`, `x` is ignored and `y` maps to an angle as `startAngle + (y / domain) × span` (domain `max` in gauge mode, total in segmented donut mode, `[min, max]` on `gauge`), centered on the ring at mid-thickness radius. `color` defaults to the chart's color (ignored for emoji, whose own colours win); `size` defaults to `2` and means dot radius, square half-side, triangle center-to-vertex radius, line half-length, or emoji font size; `line` is a small dash at the point (horizontal by default, `orientation: 'vertical'` for a vertical dash), not a full-span reference line. Annotations are purely visual (never `scene.points`, no hover/click, no a11y change), never affect scales, domains, or automatic padding, and out-of-range annotations are dropped, not clamped to an edge. Annotation marks carry `nc-annotation nc-annotation-{i}` classes. Fully backward compatible: charts without `annotations` render byte-identical scenes. Size budgets raised for the new code: core `line` standalone 4 → 4.5 kB, React `LineNanoChart` standalone 5 → 5.5 kB.
